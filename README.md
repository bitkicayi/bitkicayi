## about me

i'm studying computer engineering at Bursa Technical University, but to be completely honest, most of my days are spent having existential crises in front of a messy heap of wires, sensors, and randomly blinking LEDs on my desk. my answer to the absolute meaninglessness of the universe is building embedded systems that run purely on voltage and stubbornness: Raspberry Pi, Arduino, and UWB modules that make you ask, "what exactly are we looking at right now?"

currently, i'm with Matris Team, trying to measure how close drones can get to each other without crashing for the TEKNOFEST Swarm UAV competition. propeller-driven robots looking for coordinates in the sky... if someone asked for an absurdist theater play, you couldn't write a better script, but it is undeniably fun.

every now and then, i lift my head from the hardware world to write backend code and dig through data piles. i also happen to hold a Cisco CCNAv7 ITN certification—a wonderful piece of paper that keeps me company at 3 AM while i stare at the ceiling, questioning why an MQTT broker refuses to work.

in short: the loops are infinite, the error codes are meaningless, but as long as there's fresh filter coffee on the desk, that solder smoke is worth inhaling.
